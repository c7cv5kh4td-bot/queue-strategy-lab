MODEL RULES

What is this simulation assuming?

Estimated waiting time: 1 minute per customer + 0.1 minute per item. Each queue has one cashier and customers are served in arrival order.

Actual waiting time: Estimated time plus any hidden delay (20% chance per customer; an additional 0.5–3 minutes). The delays are created before any strategy chooses a queue and are identical across strategy comparisons.

No switching lines or new arrivals. Waiting ends when the arriving customer reaches the cashier, excluding their own checkout time. These parameters are invented for exploring the model, not measured supermarket data.
